# 💨💚 Blender 4.3 Cycles 3D Fart Blast Animations (`animations/` — All 8 Smiling Critters + Baba Chops)

Path-traced in **Blender 4.3.2 (`CYCLES` + OpenImageDenoise)** at `24 fps` (`36 frames` per cycle, `6.0s` 4× seamless loop in `.mp4` with synthesized cartoon strain squeak + brassy raspberry toot + bubbly gas pops + cheerful giggle audio).

## 🎬 4-Act Comedy Choreography (Shared Across All 9 Characters)
1. **Act 1 (`Frames 01–09` — Squat & Strain)**: Character squats deep, leans forward, squeezes both eyes shut, and trembles with effort.
2. **Act 2 (`Frames 10–14` — Big Fart Cloud Blast!)**: A multi-layered **3D green/lime/purple cartoon fart cloud** (`FartCloud.1`, `FartCloud.2`, `FartCloud.3`), expanding shockwave torus ring, and swirling stink ribbons erupt from behind.
3. **Act 3 (`Frames 15–26` — Rocket Recoil Jump)**: The force of the blast launches the critter into a cheerful mid-air hop while the toxic green puff cloud billows outward and rises.
4. **Act 4 (`Frames 27–36` — Hoof/Paw Waft & Giggle)**: Lands softly, turns toward the camera, waves one hand/hoof behind their back to fan away the lingering cloud, and giggles.
